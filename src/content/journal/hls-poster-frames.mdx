Reminder to self: HLS poster frames have to be hand-picked. Stream's auto-poster always lands on a black frame at the cut point. The fix is to pull a still at 00:00:02 and upload it as the poster explicitly.

Cost of fixing this after the fact: rebuild the page, rebuild the OG image, re-share the link. Cheaper to do it once on upload.
